Computable law for all.

The rules that decide who gets food assistance, health coverage, and tax credits live in closed code that no one outside the vendor can check. The Axiom Foundation publishes those rules in the open — cited, computable, and verified.

What we do

  1. Encode — we turn the law into software. An encoder pipeline reads a statute and drafts its encoding in RuleSpec. Every value cites its authority, every clause carries its effective dates, and each decision is recorded alongside the source text.
  2. Verify — a deterministic gauntlet decides what ships. Every draft must compile and pass its test suite to merge, and oracles cross-check results against external engines — so the model that wrote the rules never grades its own work.
  3. Publish — we put the whole chain in public. The Axiom App holds the source document, the encoding, the validation record, and the computation graph in one place, openly licensed — so a claim about what a rule computes is something you can check.

Encodings are published under CC BY 4.0 and the code under MIT — free to use, modify, and redistribute, with attribution.

What we enable

Government Stop paying to re-implement the same rules — one cited, dated, verified encoding that delivery systems, auditors, and legislators all work from.
Frontier AI labs Make AI truthful about the law — executable, cited code a model can call instead of guess, and an evaluation set for scoring what it answers unassisted.
Research A citable, executable corpus of law — effective-dated encodings cross-checked against external engines, and a computation graph that is analyzable data.
Builders Ground truth for the rules your product touches — the hardest, least differentiated layer of your stack, maintained in the open instead of by you.
